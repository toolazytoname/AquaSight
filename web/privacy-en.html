Privacy policy

Updated October 1, 2026. This policy explains how Ponder (web and iOS) handles data, where it is stored, and why it is used.

Overview

Ponder is a reading tool. We do not show ads, track you across apps or sell your data. Most features work without an account. Email and password login (with verification codes for registration and password resets) enables sync of saved items, reading records and subscriptions, and manages additional source settings.

Data stored on your device

  • Guest subscriptions, saved snapshots and reading records: in the iOS app directory with system data protection, or in browser local storage on the web;
  • Cached lists for offline reading;
  • Local language and display preferences;
  • iOS session credentials in the system Keychain, not ordinary files.

These limits apply:

  • Guest saves remain on your device while you are logged out. When you choose to log in, local saves and reading records merge into your account for sync. A new account without subscriptions also adopts locally selected basic sources; an existing account retains its account subscriptions.
  • While logged in, the session token accompanies authenticated API requests. The server uses it only for authentication and stores its hash, not the plain token. Tokens are not sent to model or advertising services.
  • You can remove saved items in the app. Uninstalling does not guarantee deletion of Keychain credentials. Log out first to remove login state; use Delete account data to also delete account data.

Data stored on the server when logged in

  • Email address: identifies your account and receives verification emails;
  • Password credentials: a salted slow hash, never the plain password. Password resets invalidate existing sessions;
  • Saved items and reading records: saved content snapshots, personal links, titles, excerpts, tags, notes and read markers for multi-device sync. These stay on the current device while logged out and are not added to public feeds or used as AI input;
  • Custom RSS feeds: the RSS/Atom URLs you add while logged in, and their article items, are stored under your account only (for multi-device sync and your reading stream); they never enter public feeds, the digest, or any other user's views. The server visits the feed addresses you provide — on your actions or a moderate staleness schedule that respects the provider's update policy (e.g. a floor of about 30 minutes) — with a standard client identifier. Feed names, addresses and item metadata (title, link, summary, date) are stored with your account data, removed when you delete the feed or your account, and are never used as AI input;
  • Account settings and feedback: subscriptions, additional-source settings and optional ranking preferences;
  • Session information: hashed tokens, along with the email, IP address and browser/client User-Agent at session creation, for login, security auditing and abuse prevention;
  • Abuse prevention counters: short-term rate limits by email and IP, subject to the deletion limits below.

We do not use this data for profiling or personalised advertising.

Content and AI summaries

Feeds use public sources, including publications, communities and GitHub. Chinese overviews are generated on the server using public source titles and summaries. Your saved items, reading records and personal data are not used as AI input. Summaries may contain omissions or errors; verify them against the original sources. English mode prefers original titles and summaries; not every article has an English translation.

When you add a link in iOS, the app may request that URL directly from your device to extract its title. Subresource loading is disabled, and the app does not store the full page, images or video. The request does not pass through our server or a model service. If it fails, the hostname is used as the title. The web app does not make this request. After login, saved links, excerpts and notes are uploaded to your own account for sync.

Hosting and third parties

  • Cloudflare hosts our API and static assets and processes necessary network requests and security records under its own policies;
  • Automatic summaries send necessary public source text to model services, without account emails, saved lists or reading records;
  • Resend (resend.com) delivers verification emails. Your email address is sent to Resend solely to deliver those messages. Support emails are also handled through ordinary email services.

The app contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK or embedded social components.

Deleting your account and data

In iOS, open Saved → Account → Delete account data and confirm. The server atomically deletes the account record, password credentials, email verification records, all sessions, saved items, reading records, settings and feedback. If deletion fails, the account remains unchanged so you can retry. After success, the app also clears that account’s local data on this device.

  • Rate-limit counters, whose keys may contain an email or IP, remain for a short window (about 48 hours) and are removed by routine maintenance;
  • Delivered verification emails cannot be recalled from your inbox. Cloudflare and Resend infrastructure logs are governed by their own policies; we cannot guarantee immediate or complete deletion;
  • Deletion does not remotely erase local copies on other devices or other accounts on the same device. Logging out removes credentials and hides that account’s saved items without deleting downloaded copies. To clear copies, clear browser data or remove the app on each device, taking the Keychain limitation above into account.
