Help & support

Answers to common questions about reading with Ponder. If you need help, email us.

Contact us

Support: lazywc@gmail.com

Describe the problem, your platform (web or iOS), and approximately when it happened. Support messages are handled through ordinary email services and used to reply to your request. For content errors or rights concerns, include the original link and a short explanation. Never send a password or verification code.

Accounts and login

How do I log in?

Use your email and password for everyday login. New users verify their email and set an 8–128 character password. If you previously used email codes, choose “Forgot password / First-time setup” and verify the same email to set a password; your saved articles and subscriptions remain. You can select basic sources, read and save articles without an account. An account enables sync and access to additional source settings on the web.

My verification email did not arrive

Codes expire after 10 minutes. Wait about 60 seconds before requesting another email, and check your spam folder. If the app reports that email delivery is temporarily unavailable, try again later. Contact lazywc@gmail.com if the problem continues.

The code is invalid or expired

Codes last 10 minutes and have a limited number of attempts. Request another email and use the newest code.

I forgot my password or have never set one

Choose “Forgot password / First-time setup” on the login screen, enter your account email, and set a new password after verification. Other devices will need to log in again. This does not delete your saved articles or subscriptions. Keep your password in a password manager and never share it or your codes.

Subscriptions and sources

How do I start reading?

Select sources in Subscriptions and save your selection to return to Reading. The starter selection offers open source and technology updates. If you select no sources, Reading shows a setup prompt; it does not automatically subscribe you to everything.

How do I enable more sources?

Log in on the web, open Subscriptions, turn on “Show more sources”, select sources and save. The iOS app refreshes your account settings automatically, or you can refresh Subscriptions manually. Use the same account on both platforms.

How do I add my own RSS feeds?

After logging in, open Subscriptions on the web or iOS, find “Custom RSS”, paste an RSS/Atom link and add it. The link is checked automatically: ordinary pages or chat invite pages that cannot be subscribed are clearly rejected, never silently saved. Articles from feeds you add join your reading stream by time; each feed can be paused, refreshed or removed individually. Custom feeds stay in your account only and never appear on the public site.

Why do some feeds ask me to wait before refreshing?

Some providers (for example AIHOT) require an update interval of about 30 minutes. To respect that, more frequent manual refreshes simply return the articles you already have, with a hint of how long to wait; nothing is lost.

Does unsubscribing remove saved articles or change notifications?

No. It changes your reading feed, but saved articles remain. Turning off additional sources hides those sources while retaining the selection for later. These actions do not enable daily digest notifications or push notifications.

Saved articles and sync

How do I save articles or add my own links?

Tap an article’s bookmark to save it. In Saved, choose Add link to save an external webpage. A valid http(s) URL is all you need. The title is optional: iOS tries to retrieve it directly on your device and uses the hostname if it cannot. It does not save the full page, images or video. The web app does not fetch webpage titles. Excerpts, tags and notes are optional. You can search saved items and manage optional information in the detail view or edit form.

Can I save articles without logging in?

Yes. Guest saves stay on the current device: in the protected app directory on iOS, or browser local storage on the web. They do not move to another device and may be lost if you clear local data.

What happens to local saves when I log in?

Local saved articles and reading records merge into your account when you log in, then sync across devices. Logging out does not delete downloaded copies; logging into the same account resumes sync. An item deleted on another device is not restored by an older local copy.

Can I read saved items offline?

You can read the fields of snapshots successfully saved on this device. This is not a full offline copy of the original webpage. Reopen the app or sync manually when online and logged in to retry pending changes. Clearing app or browser data, or uninstalling the app, affects local copies. Server saves remain available after you log in again.

Accounts and deletion

How do I delete my account data?

In the iOS app, open Saved, tap the account icon, and choose Delete account data. After confirmation, the server deletes the account record, password credentials, verification records, sessions, saved items (including personal links, tags and notes), reading records and settings in one operation. The app also clears this account’s local data on that device.

Local copies on other devices are not remotely erased. See the Privacy policy for the retention limits of rate-limit counters and infrastructure logs.

I only want to log out

Log out removes this device’s login credentials. Your server data and downloaded copies remain. To remove the account and its data, choose Delete account data.

Reading, sources and the daily digest

The website opens the public Featured edition. Featured stories, the latest news, open-source projects, the daily digest and article details are available without signing in or choosing sources first.

For a personal feed, choose sources in Subscriptions and open Reading. Guest selections and saved stories stay on this device; signing in enables account sync. The daily digest shows the public edition regardless of personal subscriptions. Custom RSS feeds and private saved items remain visible only to their owner.

Languages and content

iOS follows your system app language. The web app offers Chinese and English in Settings. Interface language does not change your subscriptions, saves or personal notes. English mode prefers original titles and source summaries; Chinese mode uses available Chinese titles and summaries. Content available only in Chinese remains in its original language.

Who writes the summaries?

Chinese overviews are generated on the server from public source titles and summaries and may contain errors or omissions. Content labelled Source summary comes from the source. Use the source links to verify the original. Rights to articles and projects belong to their respective authors.

How do I report an error or rights concern?

Email lazywc@gmail.com with the original link and a short explanation. We will review it and correct or remove the content as appropriate.
